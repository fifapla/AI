Leave Policy — Section 3.1
Full-time employees are entitled to 21 paid annual leave days per year.
Annual leave accrual begins after completing 3 months of employment.

Leave Policy — Section 4.3
Sick leave is granted for a maximum of 14 days per year.
Sick leave requires a medical certificate issued by a licensed physician.

Leave Policy — Section 4.5
Unused annual leave may be carried over to the following year, up to a maximum of 10 days.
Any balance beyond 10 days will be forfeited unless otherwise approved by HR.
