Employee Handbook — Section 5.2
Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
A flexible arrival window of up to one hour after the official start time is permitted.

Employee Handbook — Section 7.0
Resignation requires a written notice period of 30 days.
The individual employment contract may specify a different notice period, which takes precedence.

Remote Work Policy — Section 1.4
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.
Remote work arrangements require prior approval from the direct manager.
